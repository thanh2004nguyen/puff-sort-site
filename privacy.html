Puff Sort Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

This policy explains what information the Puff Sort mobile game ("the app") handles, who handles it, and the choices you have. The app is published by Nguyen Thai Thanh, an independent developer based in Vietnam ("we", "us").

Summary

  • The app has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos or location.
  • Your game progress is stored only on your device.
  • The app shows ads through Google AdMob. Google collects certain device and usage data to deliver and measure those ads.
  • We do not sell personal information.

Information stored on your device

The app saves your current level, coin balance and remaining boosters in local storage on your device. This data is not sent to us. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

Information collected by advertising partners

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob) to show banner, interstitial and rewarded ads. When ads are requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android)Ad delivery, personalization where permitted, frequency capping, fraud prevention
IP address and approximate location derived from itAd delivery, regional compliance, fraud prevention
Device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version)Ad delivery and diagnostics
Ad interactions (impressions, clicks, rewards earned)Measurement and reporting
Diagnostic data such as crash or performance data from the ads SDKStability of ad serving

Google acts under its own privacy policy. See Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices

  • Consent in Europe and the UK. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before showing personalized ads. You can change your choice later from the privacy options in the app.
  • Tracking permission on iOS. The app asks for permission before using your advertising identifier for tracking. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Settings > Privacy > Ads.
  • Personalized ads from Google. You can manage them at adssettings.google.com.

If you decline personalized ads, the app still shows ads, but they are not based on your activity.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and security

We do not operate servers that store player data. Game progress stays on your device. Data collected by Google is retained according to Google's policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold personal information ourselves, most requests about ad data need to be made to Google. We will help you with any request sent to the contact address below.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes, for example if new features or partners are added. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
